# About College

## College Name

Ethiraj College for Women (Autonomous)

## Introduction

Ethiraj College for Women is an autonomous women's institution in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, affiliated to the University of Madras, offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programmes in arts, science, commerce, management, and technology. The college was recognised as a 'College with Potential for Excellence,' the first among women's colleges in Tamil Nadu to receive this status, in 2004.

## History

- **Founding:** Founded in 1948 by Thiru V. L. Ethiraj, an eminent lawyer and philanthropist, with a founding commitment of Rs. 10 lakhs and the title deeds of two bungalows.
- **Decade 1948 1968:** Formative years introduced UG courses in Economics, Botany, Chemistry, History, Zoology, and English Literature, along with the Science Block, Hostel, Open Air Theatre, Old Library Block, and the College auditorium.
- **Decade 1968 1978:** Introduction of Commerce, Mathematics, and Physics at UG level, several PG courses, and construction of the PG block.
- **Decade 1978 1990:** Introduction of Evening College (1981); research thrust with M.Phil. and Ph.D. programmes; addition of the Annexe Campus of 59 grounds.
- **Decade 1990 2000:** Introduction of job-oriented self-funded UG courses (Corporate Secretaryship, B.Com Bank Management, BBA, B.Sc. Biochemistry, B.Sc. Microbiology, M.Sc. Plant Biology & Plant Biotechnology) and AICTE-approved PG courses MBA and MCA. New Science Block and hostel dormitories constructed.
- **2000 Onwards:** Day College became autonomous; major infrastructure growth (N & D Block, COE Block, New Library, New Hostel, Business Studies and IT Block, totaling approx. 2,66,000 sq. ft.); Language Lab and Instrumentation Centre established; departmental restructuring (Botany into Plant Biology and Plant Biotechnology, one section of History into Tourism and Travel Management, Zoology into Advanced Zoology and Biotechnology, Economics into Business Economics in self-supporting stream); Ethiraj Centre for Research, Innovation and Creativity (ECRIC) formed 2019-2020.

## Accreditation

- **Body:** National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC)
- **History:** Accorded Five Star Status in first accreditation cycle (1999). Awarded 'A' grade in second cycle (2006). Re-accredited with 'A' grade and CGPA 3.36 in third cycle (2013). Re-accredited with Grade 'A+' by NAAC Cycle IV in 2023.
- **Current Grade:** A+ (NAAC Cycle IV, 2023)
