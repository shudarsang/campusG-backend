## Institutional Scholarships

- **Provider:** Ethiraj College Trust and the Alumnae Association
- **Description:** Exclusive scholarships are offered by the Ethiraj College Trust and the Alumnae Association, rendering additional financial support to students, particularly first-generation learners, students with disabilities, and those from economically weaker backgrounds.
- **Source:** Institutional Distinctiveness document (7.3.1)

## Financial Assistance

- **Target Groups:** Nearly 50% of students are first-generation learners or come from families with low income working in the unorganised sector; students with disabilities are also prioritised for support.
- **Hostel Priority:** Priority in hostel accommodation is given to first-generation learners, students with disability, and students from humble backgrounds.
