# Mission Vision

## Vision

To achieve economic and social equity for women and through them strengthen the Nation to usher in an egalitarian society. To become a globally recognised Women's University.

## Mission

To sensitize young women to their rights and place in society through advancement and application of relevant knowledge.

## Expanded Mission Points

- To holistically develop women, by imparting knowledge, value systems, technological competencies and global skill sets.
- To harness their full potential, to prepare them to be responsible citizens, compassionate leaders, agents of social change and participants in National Development.
- To achieve our vision by creating a student-centric learning environment driven by a passion for excellence, research culture, and an eco-system for innovation and creativity.
- To encourage a participatory approach among staff, students, alumnae and other stakeholders.
- To offer a campus of excellence with world-class infrastructure, technology and support services.

## Core Values

- Empowerment of women
- Excellence in teaching and research
- Ethics and values
- Inclusivity and social equity
- Holistic and global outlook

## Founder Inspiration

Thiru V. L. Ethiraj's vision of women empowerment inspires the college to provide quality, need-based education. The college disseminates knowledge without compromising on standards and values, developing self-confidence, ethics, independent thinking, pursuit of excellence, and sensitisation to social and environmental needs.

## Institutional Objectives

- Provide quality and need-based education for women
- Foster self-confidence, ethics, and independent thinking
- Sensitise students to social and environmental needs
- Promote a holistic and global outlook on life
- Abide by all UGC regulations as enforced from time to time

## Quality Policy

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC), established in 2004, acts as the nodal unit augmenting quality in academic and administrative activities, reviewing teaching-learning processes, learning outcomes, and stakeholder feedback for continuous improvement.

## Motto

To strive, to seek, to find and not to yield
