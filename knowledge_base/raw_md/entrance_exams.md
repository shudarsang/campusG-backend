# Entrance Exams

## Accepted Entrance Exams

- **UG:** No entrance exam; admission purely on 10+2 merit
- **PG General:** No entrance exam; admission purely on UG qualifying-exam merit
- **MBA:** TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) score required, per Anna University/Tamil Nadu Govt. norms for AICTE-approved MBA programmes
- **MCA:** TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) score required, per AICTE norms for the MCA programme

## University Requirements

As an autonomous college affiliated to the University of Madras, Ethiraj College follows University of Madras regulations for degree conferment while designing its own curriculum and evaluation for UG/PG programmes under autonomy.

## Course Wise Eligibility

- **UG:** Pass in 10+2 in a relevant subject combination (course-specific, e.g., B.Com. requires Accountancy/Commerce + Mathematics/Business Mathematics + Economics/Computer Science)
- **PG General:** Graduation in the relevant or an allied discipline
- **MBA:** Any graduate degree + valid TANCET score
- **MCA:** Graduate degree with Mathematics as a subject at 10+2 or UG level + valid TANCET score

## Merit Criteria

- **UG PG Non Professional:** Admission is explicitly merit-based - ranked purely by marks secured in the qualifying examination (10+2 for UG, UG degree for PG). This is stated plainly: 'If admissions are based only on merit' applies to the vast majority of UG and PG (non-MBA/MCA) programmes at Ethiraj College.

## Interview Process

Not Available on Official Website (no interview-based selection process found in indexed pages for standard UG/PG programmes)

## Counselling Process

- **MBA MCA:** Candidates with valid TANCET scores go through a counselling process (details of counselling dates/venue announced each year on the Admissions page)
- **UG PG Non Professional:** No formal counselling process; admission proceeds directly from the merit list generated from application data
