# Placements

## Placement Cell

- **Director:** Ms./Mrs. Gomathy, Director, Placement Cell (per Ethiraj Times newsletter, September 2024)
- **Role:** Effective liaison between the industrial sector and academia; matches recruiter requirements with aspiring students; organizes an annual Career Fair followed by recruitment drives.
- **Activities:** Organizes workshops and training programmes to develop students' personality and communication skills to prepare them for the competitive job market; runs the 'Bhavishya' programme to streamline placement-related information for students.

## Internships

Internships and field visits are part of the curriculum across departments, providing hands-on training and workplace competency development, in line with the college's academic policy documented in its Self Study Report.

## Training

Personality development and communication skills training conducted by the Placement Cell; industry-institute interface maintained by departments such as MCA through membership in ICT Academy of Tamil Nadu, CSI (Computer Society of India), and Oracle Academy.

## Career Development

- **Career Fair:** Annual Career Fair conducted followed by a recruitment drive.
- **Entrepreneurship Support:** E-Cell (Entrepreneurship Cell) held its inaugural flagship event 'IDEA CONQUEST' in the Main Auditorium on 10 August (2024), attended by 354 participants; the Commerce department's E-Square cell also supports student entrepreneurship through workshops and an incubation cell.
- **Career Guidance Activities:** Guest sessions covering civil services, drone technology careers, management accountancy (Miles Education), and global finance careers (ACCA via Global FTI) have been organized as part of career awareness programmes (per September 2024 newsletter).
