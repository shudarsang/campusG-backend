# MCA Prospectus 2026-27

## MCA Programme Overview

Ethiraj College for Women offers the MCA (Master of Computer Applications) as a two-year full-time postgraduate programme in the Self-Supporting stream.

- **Started:** academic year 1999-2000
- **Duration:** 2 years, 4 semesters (2-year format as per AICTE guidelines from 2020-2021). The course must be completed within a maximum of 5 years from first-semester enrolment.
- **Affiliation:** University of Madras
- **Approval:** All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Approval No. F. No: TN-58/MCP-CS/98 dated 23 September 1998
- **Autonomy:** the MCA course got autonomous status in 2006
- **Mentor system:** every student is assigned a mentor who guides her in professional and personal life.

## MCA Eligibility Criteria (Who Can Join MCA)

Graduates from computer science, mathematics, science, commerce (B.Com), management (BBA), economics and engineering backgrounds can join MCA, as long as they studied Mathematics or Statistics as required below. Candidates who passed one of these degrees (10+2+3, 11+1+3 or 11+2+2 pattern, or Open University) are eligible for MCA:

- BCA, BES, or B.Sc in Computer Science, Mathematics, Applied Sciences, Physics or Statistics; or
- B.Com, B.B.M., B.B.A., B.L.M., B.A. Corporate Secretaryship, B.A. Economics, or any other degree with Business Mathematics and Statistics, or Mathematics/Statistics, at Main or Allied level; or
- B.Sc Chemistry with Mathematics and Physics as allied subjects; or
- B.E., B.Tech. or M.B.A.; or
- a Bachelor's degree in any discipline with Mathematics as one of the subjects at Higher Secondary (+2) level.

**TANCET 2026 marks are mandatory for MCA admission.**

## MCA Admission Procedure and Selection

- Apply online through the link on the college website and pay the non-refundable application fee of ₹600.
- The list of shortlisted candidates is published on the website, or an SMS is sent to the registered mobile number for the interview.
- **Selection (management quota):** based on UG marks, TANCET marks and performance in a Technical Interview. Interview dates are sent by e-mail.
- Selected candidates must pay the semester fee online within the stipulated time; otherwise the admission is cancelled.
- Seats are filled under Management quota and Counselling quota.

## MCA Curriculum (Syllabus)

### MCA First Year Subjects (Semester I and II)

- **Semester I:** Discrete Mathematics; Python Programming; Operating Systems and Linux Programming; Database Management Systems; Computer Laboratory I - Python Programming; Computer Laboratory II - Database Management Systems.
- **Semester II:** Advanced Java Programming; Soft Computing; Cryptography and Network Security; Computer Laboratory III - Advanced Java Programming; Computer Laboratory IV - Soft Computing.

### MCA Second Year Subjects (Semester III and IV)

- **Semester III:** Advanced Machine Learning; Digital Image Processing; Artificial Intelligence; Computer Laboratory V - Advanced Machine Learning; Computer Laboratory VI - Digital Image Processing; Computer Laboratory VII - Mini Project; Computer Laboratory VIII - Internship.
- **Semester IV:** Computer Laboratory IX - Major Project.
- **Internship:** done during the summer vacation for 4 to 6 weeks and evaluated in the following semester.

### MCA Electives

- **Semester I electives:** Advanced Software Engineering; Digital Computer Fundamentals; Web Technologies.
- **Semester II electives:** Software Quality Assurance; Cloud Computing; Cryptography and Network Security; Blockchain Technologies; Big Data Analytics; Internet of Things.
- **Semester III electives:** Mobile Application Development; Full Stack Development; Web Applications Security; Block Chain Technologies; Natural Language Processing; Virtual and Augmented Reality.
- Electives are offered at the discretion of the institution and subject to maximum registration.

### MCA Soft Skill, Value Added and Self Study Courses

- **Soft-skill courses:** Human Values and Professional Ethics; Business English Communication - Lingua Skills from Cambridge University; Technical Seminar and Report Writing; Entrepreneurial Skill Development.
- **Value-added courses:** Design the Thinking; Green Computing and Sustainable Development.
- **Self-study paper (extra credit):** Prompt Engineering and GenAI.

## MCA Bridge and Foundation Course

- Need-based bridge and foundation courses help non-major graduates meet prerequisites.
- The department's mentoring team decides, based on each student's qualifications, whether these courses are mandatory for her.
- A two-week bridge course runs before the first semester: Basics of Programming; Problem-Solving Techniques; Coding and Debugging Skills.

## MCA Student Evaluation (Marks)

Each MCA course is assessed out of 100 marks:

- **Continuous (internal) assessment:** 40 marks - internal tests (compulsory), assignments, seminars and class participation.
- **External evaluation (semester exam):** 60 marks.
- 75% attendance in every subject is required to write the semester examinations.

## MCA Industry Interface

The MCA department works directly with industry so students learn current best practices. The institution is a member of ICT Academy of Tamil Nadu, CSI (Computer Society of India) and Oracle Academy.

## MCA Infrastructure and Facilities

### MCA Computer Lab

The MCA computer laboratory has around 100 Intel Core 2 high-end computers (nodes) across the lab and faculty offices, served by Linux and Windows servers. About 90 students can work at the same time on MS Windows / UNIX platforms. The lab supports open-source Linux IDEs and software tools, Microsoft MSDN Academic Initiative licences for students, and dedicated 1:1 internet connectivity of 2 Mbps.

### MCA Library

The MCA department has its own library with more than 10,000 books plus national and international journals and e-journals. The college library adds reading rooms, an Internet Browsing Centre and access to the EBSCO and INFLIBNET research databases. Library timings: 9.00 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.

### MCA Hostel, Medical and Wi-Fi Facilities

- **Hostel:** 540 students in three buildings; apply separately at admission; first come, first served.
- **Medical and counselling:** health room run with Apollo Shine Foundation on campus; a counsellor is available during college hours.
- **Wi-Fi:** free campus-wide 24x7; students can check attendance, internal marks, exam registration and generate hall tickets online.

## MCA Vision and Mission

- **Vision:** to empower students with innovative, interdisciplinary computing knowledge and skills, fostering academic excellence, ethical leadership and technological entrepreneurship.
- **Mission:** an enriching environment for holistic personal and professional growth; promoting cutting-edge technologies in Computer Science and Applications for industry, government and the scientific community; equipping students to be successful, ethical, resourceful problem-solvers and lifelong learners.
