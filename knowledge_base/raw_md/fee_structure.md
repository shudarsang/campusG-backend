# Fee Structure

## Status

Partially Available on Official Website

## Note

The official website hosts fee-related payment links (PG-UG First Year Fees, UG Second/Third Year Fees, PG Second Year Fees, Hostel Fees, Transfer Excess Fees) via an online payment gateway, but does not publicly display a structured, itemised fee table (exact tuition/admission/semester amounts per course) on any indexed page. Actual amounts are typically shared with applicants during/after the admission process or via the payment portal, not published as open data.
