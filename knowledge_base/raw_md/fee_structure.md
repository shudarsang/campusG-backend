# Fee Structure 2026-27

## Fee Overview

Ethiraj College for Women publishes fees for the 2026-2027 academic year in its prospectuses. Fees below are per semester and come from the UG Prospectus 2026-2027 and the MCA Prospectus 2026-2027.

- **Published:** UG Self-Supporting programme fees, MCA fee, application fees, transcript fee, refund rules.
- **Not published in these documents:** UG Aided-stream fees, PG fees (M.A., M.Sc., M.Com. etc.) other than MCA, MBA fee, hostel fee.
- All fees are paid online only.

### UG Fee Notes and Additional Charges

- **ECAC charge:** ₹500, collected once in the first year, second semester.
- **Computer subject in Business Studies:** where a computer subject is offered as a Business Studies paper, an extra ₹2,500 per semester is payable.
- Programmes marked "subject to approval" depend on approval from the University of Madras.
- The cheapest Self-Supporting UG programme is B.A Business Economics with Data Analytics (₹25,531); the most expensive is B.Sc Visual Communication with AI (₹61,531).

## PG, MBA and Hostel Fees

- **PG fees (M.A., M.Sc., M.Com., M.S.W. etc.):** not published in the extracted documents. See the PG Prospectus 2026-27 on the college website.
- **MBA fee:** not published in the extracted documents. See the MBA Prospectus 2026-27.

For these fees, contact the Aided office (044-28279189) or the Self-Supporting office (044-28220822).

## Application Fees

- **UG application fee:** ₹50 (Rs. 50) per programme, paid online only, non-refundable. A separate application and fee is needed for each programme.
- **SC/ST candidates:** exempted from the application fee. The UG prospectus says the exemption is for the Aided stream; the admissions web page says Aided and Self-Supporting streams. Confirm with the college.
- **MCA application fee:** ₹600 (Rs. 600), paid online, non-refundable.

## Fee Refund Rules (Withdrawal with TC)

Refund rules for 2026-27 when a student withdraws with a Transfer Certificate (TC):

- **Within 15 days of admission or up to 10 June 2026:** 10% of the fee is deducted as processing fee, the rest is refunded.
- **Up to 25 June 2026:** 20% is deducted.
- **From 26 June 2026:** no refund.
- Refunds are credited to the bank account given in the application after the TC is approved.
- The application fee is never refunded.

## Transcript Fee

Transcript fee is ₹2,000 per copy, paid at Indian Overseas Bank (IOB), Women Entrepreneur Branch, Ethiraj College campus. Aided students use Examination Fee Account No. 1067; Self-Supporting students use Examination Fee Account No. 1175.
