# UG Prospectus 2026-27

## About the College (UG Prospectus)

Ethiraj College for Women was founded by Thiru. V. L. Ethiraj, Bar-at-Law, in 1948. It began with 169 students and is now one of the largest women's colleges in Tamil Nadu, with nearly 7,800 students, 369 faculty members and 150 non-teaching staff. The college offers 30 UG (undergraduate) courses and 26 PG courses, including the two professional courses MBA and MCA. It offered 13 M.Phil courses until 2023 and runs 10 Ph.D programmes.

## UG Admission Instructions 2026-27

### How to Apply for UG Admission

- Apply online only, through the admissions link on ethirajcollege.edu.in.
- A candidate may apply for any number of programmes in both the Aided and Self-Supporting streams.
- A separate application form and a separate fee are needed for each programme.
- **Application fee:** ₹50 per programme, paid online only, non-refundable. SC/ST candidates are exempted from the fee for the Aided stream (the admissions web page says Aided and Self-Supporting; confirm with the college).
- Give a valid, active email ID and mobile number; all communication is sent there.
- **Aadhaar:** your Aadhaar must be linked to your mobile number and must have been issued on or before 1 June 2024.

### UG Application Photo Requirements

Upload a recent passport-size photograph: maximum 100 KB, JPG or GIF format, 125 x 165 pixels. Photos in school uniform and selfies are not accepted.

### How UG Seats Are Allotted

- UG admission is on a **first come, first served** basis. There is no entrance exam for UG programmes.
- You must meet the +2 subject eligibility of the programme you apply for.
- Submitting an application does not guarantee a seat.
- An "Admission Call" message is not a confirmation of admission.
- Selected candidates must pay fees online only.
- Hostel accommodation needs a separate application after admission.

## UG Fees and Refunds

UG Self-Supporting fees for 2026-27 range from ₹25,531 to ₹61,531 per semester depending on the programme (for example BCA ₹51,531, B.Com ₹51,031, B.Sc Psychology ₹45,531). The full programme-wise fee list and the refund rules (10% deducted up to 10 June 2026, 20% up to 25 June 2026, no refund from 26 June 2026) are in the Fee Structure 2026-27. The Aided-stream UG fee table is not available in the extracted prospectus.

## UG Programmes and Eligibility 2026-27

All UG applicants need a pass in the +2 (Higher Secondary) examination from the State Board, Central Board or an equivalent board. Each programme also needs the +2 subjects listed below.

### Aided Stream B.A Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.A Economics (Aided):** any discipline (any +2 group).
- **B.A History (Aided):** any discipline.
- **B.A English (Aided):** all disciplines; preference to students with Part III / Core English.
- **B.A Tourism and Travel Management (Aided):** any discipline.

### Aided Stream B.Sc Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.Sc Mathematics (Aided):** Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.
- **B.Sc Physics (Aided):** Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.
- **B.Sc Chemistry (Aided):** Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.
- **B.Sc Plant Biology and Plant Biotechnology (Aided):** Botany, Zoology and Chemistry, or Biology and Chemistry.
- **B.Sc Advanced Zoology and Biotechnology (Aided):** Chemistry is a must; either Botany, Zoology and Chemistry with any other science subject, or Biology and Chemistry with any other science subject. Vocational subjects are not eligible.
- **B.Sc Nutrition, Food Service Management and Dietetics (Aided):** Chemistry and Biology/Botany/Zoology compulsory, plus one other listed subject (Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Nutrition etc.).

### Aided Stream B.Com Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.Com General (Aided):** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and two other listed subjects.
- **B.Com Corporate Secretaryship (Aided):** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and two other listed subjects.

### Self-Supporting Commerce and Management Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.Com (Self-Supporting):** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and two listed subjects.
- **B.Com Bank Management:** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and two listed subjects.
- **BBA:** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and two listed subjects.
- **B.Com (Hons):** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and Mathematics or Business Mathematics.
- **B.Com Accounting and Finance:** Accountancy, Commerce/Business Studies, and two non-vocational subjects.

### Self-Supporting Computer Science and Mathematics Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.Sc Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence:** Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Computer Science or Biology (subject to University of Madras approval).
- **B.Sc Cyber Security:** Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Computer Science or Biology (subject to University of Madras approval).
- **BCA:** General Mathematics, Business Mathematics or Computer Science at +2.
- **B.Sc Mathematics with Computer Application:** General Mathematics; Physics and Chemistry; plus Biology, Computer Science or Statistics.
- **B.Sc Mathematics with Data Science:** General Mathematics; Physics and Chemistry; plus Biology, Computer Science or Statistics.

### Self-Supporting Life Science, Nutrition and Psychology Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.Sc Biochemistry:** Chemistry compulsory plus another life science subject; vocational subjects not eligible.
- **B.Sc Microbiology:** Chemistry compulsory plus another life science subject; vocational subjects not eligible.
- **B.Sc Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics:** Chemistry and Biology/Botany/Zoology compulsory.
- **B.Sc Psychology:** any +2 stream; vocational students are not eligible; Science with Biology is preferred.

### Self-Supporting Arts, Economics and Media Programmes - Eligibility

- **B.A Business Economics with Data Analytics:** Commerce, Business Studies, Humanities or Science subject combination (subject to University of Madras approval).
- **B.Sc Visual Communication with Artificial Intelligence:** Science, Humanities or Commerce group (subject to University of Madras approval).
- **B.A English (Self-Supporting):** offered in the Self-Supporting stream as well; its eligibility is not listed separately in the prospectus, so confirm with the Self-Supporting office (044-28220822).

## College Timings and Working Hours (Aided and Self-Supporting)

College starts and ends at different times for the two streams:

- **Government Aided programmes:** college starts at 9.00 a.m. and ends at 2.15 p.m.; lunch break 11.45 a.m. to 12.25 p.m.
- **Self-Supporting programmes:** college starts at 11.30 a.m. and ends at 4.45 p.m.; lunch break 1.20 p.m. to 2.00 p.m. (day-order system).
- Timings may change for administrative reasons.

## UG Hostel Admission

- The college hostel houses 540 students in three buildings, for UG and PG students and research scholars.
- Apply using the separate Hostel Application Form after getting admission and paying the college fees.
- Rooms are allotted on a first come, first served basis.
