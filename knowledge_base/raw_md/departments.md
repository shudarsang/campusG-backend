# Departments

## Note

Ethiraj College runs departments under two streams: Aided and Self-Supporting (SS). Many subjects (e.g., Commerce, English, Economics, Physics) exist as separate Aided and SS departments. HOD names below are drawn from whatever official-site snippets were indexed (mostly 2022-2025 documents); treat names as indicative and verify current incumbency directly with the department for anything time-sensitive.

## Departments

### Master of Computer Applications (MCA) - Self-Supporting

- **Department Name:** Master of Computer Applications (MCA) - Self-Supporting
- **Overview:** Started in 1999, affiliated to University of Madras, approved by AICTE (Approval No. F.No: TN-58/MCP-CS/98 dated 23 September 1998). Received autonomy status in 2006. Offers a 2-year PG programme per AICTE/UGC 2020-21 guidelines and an M.Phil. in Computer Science (introduced 2014-15).
- **HOD:** Not Available (verify current HOD with department)
- **Faculty:** Not Available - full current roster not found on indexed pages
- **Laboratory:** Computer labs with Internet Browsing Centre; access to EBSCO and INFLIBNET research databases
- **Research Areas:** Not Available
- **Facilities:** Exclusive department library (10,000+ books, national/international journals, e-journals); Language Lab access; Instrumentation Centre
- **Industry Ties:** Member of ICT Academy of Tamil Nadu, CSI (Computer Society of India), and Oracle Academy; recruiters include Wipro, Infosys, TCS, HCL, Accenture, Virtusa, L&T InfoTech, Hewlett Packard
- **Contact:** Not Available
- **Source URL:** https://ethirajcollege.edu.in/mca-master-of-computer-applicationsss/

### History, Tourism and Travel Management (Aided)

- **Department Name:** History, Tourism and Travel Management (Aided)
- **Overview:** Department incepted 1948 as Department of Economics and History; upgraded to standalone History department in 1957. UG History (B.A.) since 1948; PG (M.A. History) since 1982; M.Phil. research centre since 1986; Ph.D. programme since 1988; Tourism and Travel Management (UG) branch created 2003.
- **HOD:** Dr. Sheela Kirubakaram, Associate Professor and Head (per departmental achievements page; also listed as Vice Principal - Aided in some records)
- **Faculty Sample:**
  - A.E. Jehan Kaisar, M.Phil (History)
- **Milestones:** Certificate courses in German, Spanish, Japanese, and CRS; Ethiraj College Archives introduced 2015; MoU with State Department of Archaeology (2014-15); Destination Learning introduced 2015
- **Research Areas:** Tamil Nadu History, Indian History, Women's Studies, World History, Temple Architecture, Ancient India, Tourism
- **Contact:** Not Available
- **Source URL:** https://ethirajcollege.edu.in/tourism-and-travel-management-aided/
