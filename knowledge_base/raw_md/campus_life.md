# Campus Life

- E-Square (Commerce Department's dedicated Entrepreneurship Cell)

## Cultural Activities

- **Ethi Bandhan:** 'Ethi Bandhan' - the Alumnae Association Day, observed every year on 26 January, where alumnae from all walks of life gather to relive their college memories.
- **Founders Day:** Founder's birth anniversary celebrated every year on 18 July with grandeur; 134th birth anniversary (2024) was chief-guested by Hon'ble Mr. Justice M. Sundar, Judge, Madras High Court.

## Events

- Annual Career Fair (Placement Cell)
- International Conference on Multidisciplinary Research & Innovation in Academia and Industry - recurring annual/biennial event since 2023 involving multiple departments
- IDEA CONQUEST - E-Cell's inaugural entrepreneurship event (10 August 2024)
- SYNTHAI (AI Appreciation Day) - MCA Department intra-collegiate event featuring LOLgic, Pixel War, SiteStorm, Innovision, and SymbolSense (per user's own project context; specific event dates/details to be sourced from department directly as this is a newer/smaller event not yet indexed on the public site)

## Entrepreneurship Cell

E-Cell and the Commerce department's E-Square jointly support student entrepreneurship through workshops, mentoring, speaker sessions, and an incubation cell for early-stage business ideas.

## Student Publications
