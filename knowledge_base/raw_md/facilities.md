# Facilities

## Library

- **Description:** Central library with a large collection of academic texts, reference books, national and international journals, magazines, and newspapers; conference hall, reading rooms, photocopying facility, and Internet Browsing Centre. Provides access to research databases EBSCO and INFLIBNET. Some department-level exclusive libraries also exist (e.g., MCA department library with 10,000+ books).
- **Timings:** 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. (as per one department's brochure; central library hours may vary)
- **Special Features:** Includes braille books for accessibility (per Ethiraj Times newsletter, September 2024)

## Hostel

- **Description:** College hostel with three separate blocks, housing 540 students in total. Provides gym, spacious dining halls, reading room, telecommunication facilities, and a full-time doctor.
- **Admission Policy:** First-come, first-served basis; separate hostel application required at the time of admission.
- **Priority Groups:** First-generation learners, students with disability, and students from humble backgrounds are given priority in accommodation.

## Sports

- **Description:** Training provided in basketball, volleyball, cricket, hockey, tennis, kabaddi, ball badminton, shuttle (badminton), throwball, swimming, rowing, shooting, and athletics.
- **Infrastructure:** State-of-the-art Indoor Stadium built at a cost of Rs. 5 crores, with an exclusive residential facility, promoting sports among young women.
- **Awards:** College has won awards including the Dr. Sivanthi Adityan Runner-up Trophy and the P. Hari Doss Endowment Runner-up Trophy (per third-party aggregator; not independently verified on official site).
- **Concessions:** Special concessions given to students who excel in sports.

Subject-specific laboratories across science and computer departments, including computer labs with Internet access, Language Lab, and Instrumentation Centre.

IT infrastructure well-equipped with computers and networked facilities across departments (per third-party aggregator); dedicated Internet Browsing Centre in the library.

Not Available on Official Website (specific smart-classroom infrastructure not detailed in indexed pages, though general IT/technology infrastructure is referenced across documents)

## Cafeteria

On-campus cafeteria for students and staff members.

## Medical Facilities

On-campus hospital/medical facility offering counselling and physiotherapy services; hostel has a full-time doctor.

## Auditorium

Main College Auditorium (constructed in the college's early decades, remains a landmark facility) used for events such as conferences and cultural functions.

## Placement Cell

Active Placement Cell (Director: Ms./Mrs. Gomathy, per 2024 newsletter) matching recruiter requirements with students; conducts campus recruitment drives, workshops, and training programmes for personality and communication skill development.

## Innovation Incubation Centre

Ethiraj Centre for Research, Innovation and Creativity (ECRIC), formed 2019-2020 by the Management to enhance research; E-Square Entrepreneurship Cell (Commerce Department) with an incubation cell for student business ideas.

## Research Centres

Multiple M.Phil./Ph.D. research centres across departments (e.g., History since 1986/1988); ECRIC as an overarching research promotion body.
